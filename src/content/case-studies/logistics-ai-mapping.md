## The Challenge

A regional logistics provider wanted to leverage AI but was overwhelmed by vendor pitches and unsure which opportunities would actually deliver ROI given their data maturity and team capabilities.

## Our Approach

We systematically mapped their business processes, evaluated data readiness for each potential AI application, and scored opportunities on a feasibility-value matrix.

### Key deliverables:
- Process-level mapping of 14 operational areas
- AI opportunity portfolio with 9 scored use cases
- Feasibility assessment covering data, technology, and team readiness
- Prioritised implementation roadmap with ROI projections

## The Outcome

The company launched their first AI pilot — route optimisation — within three months, achieving an 18% improvement in delivery efficiency. Two additional use cases are now in development.
