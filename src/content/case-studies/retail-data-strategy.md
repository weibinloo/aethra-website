## The Challenge

A growing retail company with 12 locations was struggling with fragmented data across multiple POS systems, inventory tools, and marketing platforms. Leadership knew they were missing opportunities but couldn't get a clear picture of their business.

## Our Approach

We conducted a rapid assessment of their data landscape, identified the highest-value integration points, and designed a phased data strategy that prioritised quick wins while building toward a unified data platform.

### Key deliverables:
- Data maturity assessment across all business units
- Target-state architecture for a unified customer and inventory data platform
- Governance framework with clear data ownership and quality standards
- 12-month phased roadmap prioritised by business impact

## The Outcome

Within six months of beginning implementation, the company achieved a 30% reduction in stockouts through better demand forecasting and a 15% improvement in marketing ROI through unified customer segmentation.
